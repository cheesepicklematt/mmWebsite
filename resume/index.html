Selected work

Execution Stack, Sydney Quant

2024 – present

The private crypto trading system the business runs on. It trades unattended and is profitable.

  • Continuous market data capture over exchange WebSockets and REST APIs, stored raw and append-only so any past decision can be re-examined against exactly what the system saw.
  • Medium-frequency strategies developed and backtested across mean reversion, market making, Bayesian parameter optimisation and portfolio selection.
  • One code path for backtest and live: the backtester is the live engine fed historical data through the same interface, with costs, latency and slippage modelled rather than assumed away.
  • Idempotent order submission and an explicit order state machine, so a retry after a timeout can never become a second position. Rejects and partials are handled as normal cases, not exceptions.
  • Pre-trade limits on position, exposure, order rate and drawdown, checked before submission. Reconciliation against the venue on every start, reconnect and interval.
  • Kill switch that fails closed on stale data or disconnection, and deterministic replay of any session from the log.
  • Python
  • Async IO
  • WebSockets
  • PostgreSQL
  • Time-series
  • AWS

Footylab, pricing and edge detection

2025 – present

Market-making thinking applied to sports betting markets: build an independent fair price, de-vig the offered market, and trade the gap.

  • Independent probability estimate from historical form and matchup data, compared against the bookmaker’s true implied probability once the overround is stripped out.
  • Edge measured in percentage points against explicit, versioned thresholds. The rules live in configuration and are applied uniformly, not adjusted per market.
  • Scheduled collection of fixtures, form and market prices into MySQL, with the full working attached to every verdict.
  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • MySQL
  • Scheduled pipelines
  • Next.js

Portfolio Lab, look-through exposure

2025 – present

Resolves a portfolio of ETFs to the single-name exposure actually held underneath.

  • Parses published issuer holdings disclosures from BetaShares and iShares and nets them into one look-through position, surfacing concentration and overlap that the fund labels hide.
  • Pairwise overlap and top-10 concentration metrics, with disclosure coverage reported rather than assumed, so you can see what the portfolio is genuinely exposed to rather than what the fund labels imply.
  • Python
  • Holdings parsers
  • Flutter
